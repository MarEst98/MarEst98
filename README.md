Mario E. Avila-Andrade

Economist working on financial economics and macro-finance. Applied econometrics, text as data, and large-scale financial and administrative datasets.

Previously a pre-doctoral researcher at the AQR Asset Management Institute, London Business School. Master's (Research) in Financial Economics from University College London. Before that, I worked in Latin American venture capital and private capital research.

Research interests

Information frictions and disclosure in asset markets
Credit, collateral and financial intermediation
Private capital markets
Methods: causal inference, text as data / NLP, stochastic choice, structural simulation

Tools: Python · Julia · R · Stata · MATLAB · SQL · LaTeX. Data: WRDS (CRSP, Compustat, TAQ), SEC filings, linked administrative microdata.

Selected work

Textual Analysis of Corporate Disclosures and Stock Returns: Evidence from Recent U.S. Data. Working paper (UCL, 2026)
Causal Evaluation and Stochastic Revealed Preferences with Unobserved Randomness (with N. Chuquimarca). Working paper (2023)
Macroeconomic Implications of the COVID-19 Pandemic: An Analysis of Early Academic Production. Cuestiones Económicas, 31(2), 2021
